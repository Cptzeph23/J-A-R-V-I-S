# ADR-009: Hosted STT/TTS first
Date: 2026-10-20 | Status: Proposed

Context: Accuracy and battery matter; continuous listening is restricted on mobile.
Alternatives: On-device STT/TTS, hybrid.
Decision: Push-to-talk with hosted STT/TTS; on-device TTS as fallback; wake word deferred (P3) and foreground-only.
Consequences: Network dependency and per-use cost; failure recovery required. Provider chosen in Phase 12 after pricing and retention review.
